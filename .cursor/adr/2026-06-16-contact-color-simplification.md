# ADR: Contact Color Simplification

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-06-16
- Owners: Portfolio maintenance

## Context
The contact section used multiple accent tones at once, which made the card feel visually noisy compared to the rest of the page.

## Decision
- Reduce contact section to a tighter blue palette.
- Keep one primary accent for links and one secondary accent for default link text.
- Simplify contact card background and border to lower visual saturation.

## Consequences
### Positive
- Cleaner, more professional contact area.
- Better consistency with overall site palette.

### Trade-offs
- Less color variation and decorative contrast.

## Alternatives Considered
- Keep multicolor accents (rejected: too busy).
- Remove all accents from links (rejected: weak interaction affordance).
